### **Revised & Final Plan for Week 1 Live "Consulting Workshop" (180 Minutes)**

**Session Title:** From Blank Page to Custom AI: Building Your First System Prompt

**Core Objectives:**
*   To model the intellectual "struggle" of creating a professional R.A.F.T. prompt from scratch.
*   To facilitate the strategic formation of cross-functional student teams.
*   To provide a hands-on workshop where students build their first practical artifact: a System Prompt for a custom AI.
*   To introduce and reinforce professional habits like iterative optimization and prompt versioning.

---

### **Agenda & Detailed Activity Breakdown**

#### **Part 1: Team Formation & The "Blank Page" Problem (60 minutes)**

*   **(0:00 - 0:15) — 15 mins: Welcome, Agenda & The "Why" of Our Teams**
    *   **Instructor's Activity:** Welcome students. Outline the session's agenda: "Today is about building. We'll build our teams, and then we'll build our first professional-grade AI instruction together."
    *   The instructor explains the **"cross-functional team" philosophy** based on the pre-course survey: "In consulting, you don't choose your team; you are placed in a team with diverse skills. We are replicating that professional environment. Your goal is to learn how to leverage each other's strengths."

*   **(0:15 - 1:00) — 45 mins: Strategic Team Formation Workshop**
    *   **Instructor's Activity:**
        1.  The instructor presents the pre-determined teams based on the survey results.
        2.  Each team is moved to a breakout room.
    *   **Student Activity: A combination of **speed dating** and **sports drafting**.

---

#### **Part 2: The Core Skill: Building a System Prompt (60 minutes)**

*   **(1:00 - 1:30) — 30 mins: Live Demo - The "Struggle" of Building a R.A.F.T. Prompt**
    *   **Instructor's Activity:**
        1.  The instructor shares their screen with a blank text editor.
        2.  **Frame the Problem:** "In the pre-recorded video, you saw the perfect final prompt. But how do we get there? Let's build a **System Prompt** for a Custom GPT from scratch. Our goal: create an 'HR Policy Bot' that can answer employee questions reliably."
        3.  **The "Struggle" (Thinking Aloud):** The instructor will model the iterative process, starting with a bad prompt and refining it.
            *   "Okay, let's start with just the **# Task**. `Your task is to answer questions about HR policy.` That's too simple. What if an employee asks for a raise?"
            *   "We need a **# Role** and guardrails. `You are an HR assistant.` Better. Let's add: `You must only answer questions based on the provided documents.`"
            *   "What about the **# Audience**? Employees. The tone should be helpful but professional. And the **# Format**? For a chatbot, simple text is fine, but we need it to cite its sources. Let's add: `At the end of each answer, you must cite the document and page number you used.`"
        4.  The instructor assembles the complete R.A.F.T. System Prompt live, showing how each component is a solution to a potential problem.

*   **(1:30 - 2:00) — 30 mins: Hands-On Workshop - Build Your Own System Prompt**
    *   **Instructor's Activity:** "Now it's your turn. In your new teams, you will build a System Prompt for a different Custom AI. Your task: **Design a 'Marketing Copy Co-Pilot'.**"
    *   **The Specific Task for Teams:** "Your System Prompt must define the AI's **Role** (e.g., expert copywriter), its target **Audience** (e.g., marketing managers), and a clear **Format** for its output (e.g., 'Always provide three variations of any copy request: one professional, one casual, and one witty'). Use a shared document to build your R.A.F.T. prompt together."
    *   **Instructor's Role (Consulting Rounds):** The instructor will circulate, providing feedback: "That's a good start for the Role, but can you make it more specific? What *kind* of copywriter is it? For the Format, how will you ensure the three variations are clearly labeled?"

---

#### **Part 3: Discussion & Professional Practices (60 minutes)**

*   **(2:00 - 2:30) — 30 mins: Discussion & Team Share-out**
    *   **Instructor's Activity:** Facilitate a discussion where teams share their "Marketing Copy Co-Pilot" System Prompts.
    *   **Student Activity:** One or two teams will share their final prompt. The instructor will facilitate a class-wide discussion comparing the different approaches.
        *   **Prompting Questions:** "Team A chose the Role 'Brand Voice Expert,' while Team B chose 'Direct Response Copywriter.' How would that change the output? Which team's Format instruction was clearer? Why?"

*   **(2:30 - 2:45) — 15 mins: Naming Professional Practices**
    *   **Instructor's Activity:**
        1.  **Iterative Optimization:** "The 'struggle' you saw me go through, and that you just experienced in your teams—that is **iterative prompt optimization**. It's the core professional workflow."
        2.  **Prompt Versioning:** "As you build these prompts, start the habit of **prompt versioning** now. Save your team's System Prompt as `marketing_co-pilot_v1.0.txt`. This is a non-negotiable professional practice."

*   **(2:45 - 3:00) — 15 mins: Final Q&A and Look Ahead**
    *   **Instructor's Activity:** Open the floor for any remaining questions about Week 1's content.
    *   **Final Look Ahead:** "Excellent work building your teams and your first professional artifact. You now have the foundational skills. Be ready, because next week, the real work begins. You will receive your official course project and your first milestone deliverable."
